Cmd+Z and Cmd+Shift+Z, several steps back. A safety net for the edit you regret a second later, not a second history: it survives a refresh, goes when the tab closes, and writes nothing to .chocks. Undoing a delete restores the whole subtree with its original uids, so links keep working.
